On this site, the receipts show how that simple training objective scales from GPT-1 into systems that write, reason, code, persuade, and begin collapsing the line between autocomplete and general-purpose cognition.
ChatGPT is not a single magic box. It is the consumer-facing layer built on top of GPT-class models, instruction tuning, conversation handling, and deployment infrastructure. The site shows the sequence clearly: GPT-1 proves the blueprint, GPT-3 makes broad language performance unavoidable, then ChatGPT turns frontier research into mass behaviour. Whether the term is precise is less important than the fact that powerful actors are acting as though it is reachable.
